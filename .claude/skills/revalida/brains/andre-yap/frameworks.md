# Andre Yap: frameworks

Frameworks Andre uses, teaches, and operationalizes. Every framework has the source id it came from. Quotes are verbatim from his own sites and the 100X OS documentation.

## GPS: Genius, Purpose, Service (S002)

The Ignite Manifesto's spine, which he runs as an operating system ("operationalize everyday Greatness in every person, team, venture and organization we Ignite").

1. "I am gifted with my own Genius -- unique, unrepeated and unrepeatable for all time."
2. "my singular Genius bears the thumbprint of Who I Am and Why I am Here. Genius points to Purpose, Purpose to Genius."
3. "I can only give out of what I have been given. And what I have been given, I must give and live for others. Genius finds its fullness in Service -- a Purpose bigger than self."
4. Honest and brave people find "their manifest call in a shared Mission -- with others, for others. A Mission rooted in finding that particular slice of humanity whose particular pains I am particularly gifted (and compelled!) to heal."
5. "shared Mission leads naturally to Collaboration. Shared Mission demands Shared Genius: mine, yours, ours."
6. From there to Innovation and Impact: "We Ignite, therefore, not just for self, but for each other."

The path: "from a sense of our own individual Genius, Purpose, and Service (GPS) toward shared Mission and Collaboration, and ultimately, to Innovation and Impact."

His shorthand for the manifesto: "To many thousands of professionals who experience the Manifesto, they call it, simply: GPS."

## The 12 Meta-Skills (S007)

His words, verbatim definitions:

1. **FIRST PRINCIPLES**: "Strip away inherited assumptions and reason from what is actually true, necessary and causal."
2. **POWER LAW**: "Find and concentrate on the few variables, customers, problems and actions capable of disproportionately changing the outcome."
3. **CUSTOMER OBSESSION**: "Let the customer's lived reality -- not internal assumptions -- govern what gets built, changed and ultimately valued."
4. **RADICAL OUTCOMES**: "Define success by consequential change in reality -- not activity, completion, output or incremental improvement."
5. **GROUND COMMANDO**: "Get close enough to reality to see what dashboards, abstractions and intermediaries miss -- and act on it."
6. **RADICAL CANDOR**: "Confront consequential truth -- including inconvenient truth -- without protecting ego, narrative or hierarchy."
7. **LEARNING ACUITY**: "Convert reality's feedback into better models, decisions and changed behavior -- fast enough to compound."
8. **EXECUTION AGILITY**: "Move decisively from insight to action, while changing course quickly when reality changes the answer."
9. **SYSTEMS THINKING**: "See how actors, incentives, feedback loops and consequences interact -- and intervene where the system can actually move."
10. **WHOLE PERSON**: "Build consequentially without hollowing out the human being doing the building."
11. **FOLLOW THE GOOD**: "Recognize what is genuinely working or consequential -- and put increasing energy behind it."
12. **DEEPLY PERSONAL LEADERSHIP**: "Lead from personal ownership, conviction and example in ways that increase the consequential capacity of other people."

## The 6 Build Stacks (S007)

The build journey, in order, each with its canonical capability:

1. **NUKE NOW!** -- "Can he find the right problem to solve? Can Albert cut through complexity, identify the customer problem that matters most and make it small enough to attack decisively?"
2. **ENTRY MICROCOSM** -- "Can he build something customers genuinely use and value? Can Albert turn the right problem into a focused solution that drives the right usage, produces a meaningful outcome and creates enough value to justify what the customer gives in return?"
3. **VALUE MACROCOSM** -- "Can he turn an initial solution into a larger business opportunity? ...recognize where proven customer value can propagate into additional customers, stakeholders and value pools without confusing possible expansion with earned expansion?"
4. **CAPTURE ECONOMICS** -- "Can he turn the value he creates into durable revenue and profit? ...identify who receives enough value to pay, how value is captured across the relationship and whether the resulting economics compound over time?"
5. **EFFICIENT SCALE** -- "Can he grow the business without growth costs outrunning its returns? ...acquire and serve substantially more customers while preserving -- or improving -- the value and economics of each additional customer?"
6. **100X ROI + IMPACT** -- "Can he compound all of this into extraordinary business results and real-world impact?"

## The converge-diverge learning loop (S005)

How population-scale problem solving works: "CONSEQUENTIAL CHALLENGE -- CONVERGE PEOPLE AROUND IT -- DIVERGE INTO THOUSANDS OF ATTACKS -- REALITY ANSWERS BACK -- LEARN INDIVIDUALLY + COLLECTIVELY -- CONVERGE ON WHAT REALITY IS TEACHING -- RE-DIVERGE INTO BETTER ATTACKS -- repeat."

His thesis on the central problem: "The central problem of population-scale problem solving is not how to generate more ideas, activity or learning. It is how to preserve productive divergence while continuously converting distributed encounters with reality into collective intelligence -- and returning that intelligence to each individual as a better next move."

## Population-scale problem solving as institution (S005)

"Most institutions organize people around courses, jobs, departments, credentials and organizations -- not around consequential problems themselves. EXPEDITION asks what happens when the consequential problem becomes the organizing unit, and education, work, AI, talent, capital and institutions assemble around it."

The ladder: "PROBLEM-SOLVING PLATFORM -- PROBLEM-SOLVING INSTITUTION -- PROBLEM-SOLVING CIVILIZATION."

The experiment: "can 25,000 radically different people become more capable individually because they are solving consequential problems collectively -- and can society become better at attacking its hardest problems because those people exist?"
