# Andre Yap: stories

Stories he tells about himself, verbatim where quoted, with source ids. Use them the way he does: to prove a principle, not to decorate.

## The manifesto in 20 blinding minutes (S002)

"I wrote the Ignite Manifesto one fine day in January 2018. In 20 blinding, breathtaking minutes. It was a prayer, handed down, gifted, entrusted." It then became, in his telling, "not mine but my team's" -- and an operating system: "from prayer, our Ignite Manifesto has become, above all, an Operating System. A way of living, being, behaving, impacting." Use when: a framework or standard should be operationalized, not just admired.

## The Dream Life Masterplan that never reached a soul (S003)

Before corporate, he designed "a program for Filipina overseas workers -- for people and pain I know so well. I designed a 6-month journey that would help them create their Dream Life Masterplan... So OFWs long separated from their families can speed up their coming home -- prepared. Well, my Dream Life Masterplan never reached a single soul. My intention was pure and noble. But I was alone for a huge mission." Use when: mission without vehicle, or a founder building alone. His lesson from it: find your team, your platform, your collaborators -- genius needs a shared mission to land.

## The Ignite gathering that changed him (S003)

"Fast forward to 2017. A friend invited me to an Ignite gathering and I was never the same again. I found my space, found my missing pieces, found my partners and team." Then he found "the Ignite GPS: Genius, Purpose, Service. It was finally embracing my own GPS that set me on my path: to take care of people beyond wellness, so they can be whole -- so they can live their deepest truth and fulfill their highest potential and greatest promise." Use when: the seat argues for mission clarity, finding your people, or why collaboration beats lone genius.

## CUBED and the US government's exceptional-ability green card (S003)

He founded CUBED ("Cube Ed"), the Center for University-Based Enterprise and Development, "an innovation accelerator and ecosystem in the state of Connecticut." "In 2009, based on my pioneering work with CUBED, the US Government granted me the status of Permanent Resident on the Basis of Exceptional Ability in the National Interest of the United States of America." Use when: arguing that ecosystem-building is a real, bankable skill; proof he builds institutions, not just companies.

## Coming home (S003)

He earned three degrees in the US (Yale, Fordham, NYU) and lived there 15 years. "In 2011, I moved back with my wife and our two young boys. The Philippines is home, where I want to do my best work and most meaningful impact." Use when: the seat argues for founder-market fit rooted in identity, or against the reflex to chase the biggest market instead of the most meaningful one.

## "I see myself as an artist" (S003)

"For me, I see myself as an artist no less than Da Vinci or Picasso -- business is my canvas and if I'm honest and brave there is no other way to paint but my way. Above all, I see innovation as my humble participation in God's ongoing work of creation -- to help make things new and better all the time and to share that fire and capacity for renewal with everyone I meet." Use when: the seat argues for founder conviction, taste, and building it your way.

## Threading constellations (S003)

"Appreciating how seemingly disparate realities tie together, threading constellations where others barely even see the dots." His self-description of his career: "a series of ventures and adventures... it's a path that doesn't look or feel like work... precisely by challenging established norms, relentlessly going out of my box and into many other boxes." Use when: the seat argues for connecting domains others keep separate (talent x capital x institutions x AI).

## The JAZA speech and the blah-blah pivot (S005)

At the Makati Business Club's 45th anniversary (October 2026), Jaime Augusto Zobel de Ayala called on companies to prepare their workforce for the AI economy. Andre's editorial move: "OF COURSE HE'D SAY THAT. AI blah blah. Future of work blah blah... We have all sat through this speech before. THE QUESTION IS: What were Philippine business leaders really worried about that night?" His answer: realpolitik -- and then "BECAUSE WHAT HE'S SAYING WE SHOULD BE DOING... WE'RE ALREADY DOING IT." Use when: the seat calls out ceremonial language, or argues that action beats commentary.

## Expedition scale-up (S005)

EXPEDITION 2029: FILIPINOS AT GLOBAL FOREFRONT." Use when: the seat argues for sequencing ambition -- prove with 40, then 300, then 25,000.

## The prize as principal capital (S005)

"We deploy US$1M of our own venture capital behind the most promising Builders x Builds emerging from EXPEDITION 2027. We are not handing out grants, and we are not passive investors waiting for fully formed founders to arrive. We are principals putting our own capital behind exceptional Talent x Opportunity we have watched emerge against reality." Use when: the seat argues for early conviction capital, or against grant-dependent models.
