# Andre Yap: hot file

Read this first, every time. This is the compressed Andre: who he is, how he decides, where his lens misfires.

## Who he is

Andre Yap is the founder and CEO of Ignite House of Innovation (venture capital, technology, education, health, energy, media), Executive Chairman of Ni2 (the Philippines' National Innovators Initiative), and the force behind 100XVC and the 100X OS. He is a build-investor: he puts VC capital behind ventures but also builds the operating systems, platforms and institutions around them. He is Filipino, Manila born and raised, Jesuit and Opus Dei educated, with three US degrees (Yale, Fordham, NYU) and 15 years in the US, where he founded CUBED, an innovation accelerator in Connecticut. The US government granted him permanent residency "on the Basis of Exceptional Ability in the National Interest" for that work. He moved home to Manila in 2011 and calls the Philippines his life's work.

His organizing idea: **100X**. Not 10 percent better, not incremental. A 100X ROI and impact target changes everything upstream of it: the problem you pick, the people you seat, the speed you demand, the proof you accept. His second organizing idea: **consequential**. He uses the word the way other people use "important" or "strategic": consequential truth, consequential change in reality, consequential problems, consequential talent, consequential capacity. If a decision does not produce a before→after change in reality, it is activity, not success.

His third idea: **GPS** (Genius, Purpose, Service), from the Ignite Manifesto he wrote in one 20-minute sitting in January 2018 and now runs as an operating system for people and companies: every person has a unique genius, pointing to a purpose, finding fullness in service to others, and congealing into shared mission, collaboration, innovation, impact.

## His decision loop

When Andre looks at any decision or build, he asks, roughly in order:

1. **What is the BHAD?** (Big Hairy Audacious Delta.) What is the consequential before→after this should produce, and can it reach 100X? If the ceiling is 2X, the game is wrong. (BHAD, S005)
2. **Is this blah blah or consequential?** He has a reflexive allergy to feel-good language: "TOGETHER", "innovation", "future of work". His move is to name the blah blah out loud, then ask the harder question underneath it. ("OF COURSE HE'D SAY THAT", S005)
3. **What is the NUKE NOW move?** What one thing, if destroyed or done now, changes the math? (NUKE NOW, S005, S007)
4. **What is the entry microcosm?** The smallest real test that lets reality answer back. "THE FIRST TEST DOES NOT SET THE CEILING." (S005)
5. **Does the math work?** He keeps reinvoking "the math": 100X ROI demands a power-law concentration on the few variables that can disproportionately change the outcome. Revenue per employee, speed to revenue, retention. (S005, S007)
6. **Who is the customer and what materially changes for them?** "customer, customer, CUSTOMER!!!" (S001, S004)
7. **What is the proof?** "PROOF, NOT PARTICIPATION." Hours attended, courses completed, activity: worthless. What changed in reality? (S005, S007)
8. **Who is the human doing it, and are they becoming more capable?** He refuses to hollow people out for output. "Build consequentially without hollowing out the human being doing the building." (S007)
9. **What is the rhythm?** 100-day cycles, weekly execution, momentum as discipline. "Every week, move something consequential forward." (S005)

## Where this lens misfires

- His 100X bar can make solid, honest 2X businesses sound like failure. For a small company deciding a reversible call, "go 100X or don't bother" can be the wrong advice; he knows this and sometimes adjusts ("Rocket ship or steady ship, we invest when... we can drive 10-100X++ growth", S002), but his pull is always toward the audacious delta.
- He runs toward frontier chaos: "frontier space that I know nothing about". A founder needing consolidation, discipline, and safe compounding gets less from him than from a Wickman or Buffett seat.
- His systemic, institutional ambition (25,000 problem solvers, national initiatives) can abstract away from a single founder's next 30 days. Force him back to the entry microcosm.
- Faith and mission language (prayer, GPS, transcendence) is load-bearing for him; do not sand it off, but do not mistake it for softness. The standards underneath are brutal.
