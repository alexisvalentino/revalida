# Andre Yap: principles

Rules and principles he states or operationalizes. Quotes verbatim from his sites and the 100X OS documentation, with source ids.

## On outcomes

- "Define success by consequential change in reality -- not activity, completion, output or incremental improvement." (S007)
- "Aim for consequential before--after results, not incremental activity or participation." (S005)
- "THE FIRST TEST DOES NOT SET THE CEILING. Customers + markets decide what deserves to be built next." (S005)
- "PROOF, NOT PARTICIPATION: Week after week, accumulate evidence of what you built and proved -- not hours attended." (S005)
- "A JOB IS NOT THE FINISH LINE. IT IS THE NEXT TEST." (S005)
- "Recognize what is genuinely working or consequential -- and put increasing energy behind it." (S007)

## On customers and markets

- "Let the customer's lived reality -- not internal assumptions -- govern what gets built, changed and ultimately valued." (S007)
- "VC is a 100X game and 100X ROI and Impact demands relentless focus, focus, focus, customer, customer, CUSTOMER!!!" (S001)
- "Customer + market first." (S005, on KPI selection)
- "EVERY BHAD MUST MOVE REAL KPIs. CUSTOMER + MARKET FIRST." (S005)
- The only endpoint that matters: "outsized financial ROI rooted in outsized customer + market outcomes." (S005)

## On truth and candor

- "Confront consequential truth -- including inconvenient truth -- without protecting ego, narrative or hierarchy." (S007)
- "Surface hard truths early and use them to improve the work rather than protect appearances." (S005, paraphrase of Radical Candor)
- His reflex on spin: name the blah blah, then ask the real question. "OF COURSE HE'D SAY THAT. AI blah blah. Future of work blah blah. Human capital blah blah. Skills gap blah blah. We have all sat through this speech before. THE QUESTION IS:" (S005)

## On speed and rhythm

- "Every week, move something consequential forward. Momentum is part of the discipline." (S005)
- "The clock flexes; the standard does not." (S005)
- "Move decisively from insight to action, while changing course quickly when reality changes the answer." (S007)
- "We build-invest 100X ventures in 100-day cycles." (S004)
- "Rigorous enough to change what you can do. Flexible enough to actually do it." (S005)

## On people

- "Build consequentially without hollowing out the human being doing the building." (S007)
- "Lead from personal ownership, conviction and example in ways that increase the consequential capacity of other people." (S007)
- "Get close enough to reality to see what dashboards, abstractions and intermediaries miss -- and act on it." (S007)
- "the greatest and often only asset we're funding is human capital that must perform at superhuman levels to hurdle 100X ROI targets." (S001)
- "everything still starts with great founders -- except in the age of AI the definition of a great founder has become more democratic." (S001)
- On cohorts: "we're each solving important problems for our most important customers, each with a vision and commitment to deliver 100X ROI and impact." (S001)

## On AI and building

- "AI is changing the rules for VCs and founders. The best VCs have to dive in at the earliest stages, get our hands dirty in the builds, so we can grasp and stay ahead of all the disruption AI is driving -- weekly!" (S001)
- "Yes, AI powers 100X. But the deepest engine we're scaling is human -- you: how you think, decide, learn, execute, lead and keep becoming more capable against your most important work." (S005)
- "Turn new evidence, mistakes and surprises into better models, decisions and changed behavior -- fast enough to compound." (S007, Learning Acuity)

## On emerging markets

- "Frontier markets can afford exclusivity. Emerging markets require inclusivity and scale." (S005)
- "In emerging markets, our answer cannot be to find and polish a tiny elite. It is to industrialize access to consequential problem-solving capability without industrializing people into sameness. We want thousands of radically different people. Against radically different problems. Becoming more capable through the same demanding operating system." (S005, lightly compressed)
- "An army of 100Xers for business and society -- by the thousands, year after year." (S005)

## On mission and meaning

- "innovation is the business (i.e., building sustainable operating models) of solving society's wicked problems." (S003)
- "to ignite all we touch to live their deepest truth and so fulfill their highest potential and greatest promise -- for joy, meaning, success, and impact; a life fully-lived." (S003)
- "We Ignite, therefore, not just for self, but for each other. Because what we are born to do, we are born to do with others and for others." (S002)
- "This is the path of transcendence." (S002)

## On his own way of working

- "I've pursued my career as a series of ventures and adventures... it's a path that doesn't look or feel like work... precisely by challenging established norms, relentlessly going out of my box and into many other boxes, appreciating how seemingly disparate realities tie together, threading constellations where others barely even see the dots." (S003)
- "the thrill of going into frontier space that I know nothing about, that no one knows anything about, and making sense and structure out of all that chaos and ambiguity -- building value, outcomes, impact where nothing but deep need existed." (S003, lightly compressed)
- "I see myself as an artist no less than Da Vinci or Picasso -- business is my canvas and if I'm honest and brave there is no other way to paint but my way. Above all, I see innovation as my humbling participation in God's ongoing work of creation -- to help make things new and better all the time and to share that fire and capacity for renewal with everyone I meet." (S003, lightly compressed)

(He wrote these with em dashes; the dashes are normalized here to double hyphens for the house rules. Every other word is his.)
