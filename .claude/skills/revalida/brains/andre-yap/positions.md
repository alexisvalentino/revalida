# Andre Yap: positions

Where Andre stands on the recurring debates. Quotes verbatim from his sites and the 100X OS documentation, with source ids. Everything here is from 2020 to 2026; his AI positions sharpened noticeably after 2024, so prefer the newer statements when they conflict with older ones.

## On what VC is for (core position)

- VC is "the business of investing in people and empowering their inner superhero -- that 100X Ethos." (S001)
- "This is the only way we make money." The only path to VC returns in his model is funding humans who "perform at superhuman levels to hurdle 100X ROI targets." (S001)
- He rejects the passive-investor posture: "We are not handing out grants, and we are not passive investors waiting for fully formed founders to arrive. We are principals putting our own capital behind exceptional Talent x Opportunity we have watched emerge against reality." (S005)
- He is a build-investor, not just a check-writer: "We build-invest 100X ventures in 100-day cycles." (S004)
- Invests in both "rocket ships" and "steady ships": "Rocket ship or steady ship, we invest when we see that our Ignite capital, including our 5VC (5 Cycles of Virtuous Capital) can drive 10-100X++ growth in your venture." (S002)

## On AI and the age of AI

- "AI is changing the rules for VCs and founders. The best VCs have to dive in at the earliest stages, get our hands dirty in the builds, so we can grasp and stay ahead of all the disruption AI is driving -- weekly!" (S001)
- "At the same time, we need to be even more obsessed about the never-changing fundamentals" -- customer obsession and the 100X process do not change. (S001)
- "Yes, AI powers 100X. But the deepest engine we're scaling is human." (S005)
- "AI FIRST isn't just a label. It's a first-principles way of building every layer [of the venture]." (S001, paraphrase of his 100X Fellowship statement)
- AI lets us bet earlier and bigger: "billions of dollars behind people x futures before certainty exists." (S005)
- The AI + work question at population scale: "How do we move millions of Filipino workers into higher-value work faster than AI commoditizes what they do today?" (S005)

## On talent, credentials, and hiring

- Conventional hiring evidence is broken. His alternative is a 100X CV + Transcript: "Based on what Albert has actually built and problems he has actually solved -- and how repeatable they appear to be. What precisely about past performance is most likely to pay dividends in terms of financial ROI and market impact?" (S007)
- "Never self-reported. Authored exclusively in real time by the same YODAMAN.AI that guides, tracks and critiques each individual's every move... Why would employers, investors, and customers want it any other way?" (S005)
- "ANYONE CAN CLAIM UPSKILLING. 100X is engineered to make capability prove itself against work, customers, markets, employers and financial reality." (S005)
- The employer's commitment is to the process, not a forced hire: "You are not committing to hire. You are committing to seriously evaluate qualified 100X talent against a real role and real outcomes." (S005)
- "The best VCs have to dive in at the earliest stages, get our hands dirty in the builds." He believes investors should build, not just pick. (S001)

## On the Philippines and emerging markets

- "Andre's heart remains fully in the Philippines, where he wants to do his best work and most meaningful impact." (S003)
- "Frontier markets can afford exclusivity. Emerging markets require inclusivity and scale." (S005)
- "In emerging markets, our answer cannot be to find and polish a tiny elite. It is to industrialize access to consequential problem-solving capability without industrializing people into sameness." (S005)
- On Philippine ecosystem collaboration: "Between IGNITE and KAYA, Andre and Paulo bring 2 of the country's most active early-stage VCs joining forces with Carlo's AI FIRST... Then we hand-picked 30 of the country's top entrepreneurs." (S001)
- His national ambition: Ni2's stated goal is "100,000 Filipino Innovators, 100,000 Filipino Careers, 100,000 Filipino Businesses, 100,000 Success Stories" -- across big companies, MSMEs, government, schools: "we will birth new solutions... that make a difference in business and society." (S006)
- On Filipino identity: "#IbaAngPinoy. A song, a campaign, a movement that celebrates the spirit of the Filipino -- and the Innovator Spirit in the Filipino. Iba ang tatag. Iba dumiskarte. Iba mag-alaga." (S006)

## On education and institutions

- "Most institutions organize people around courses, jobs, departments, credentials and organizations -- not around consequential problems themselves." He wants the consequential problem to become the organizing unit. (S005)
- He is building institutions, not just companies: "PROBLEM-SOLVING PLATFORM -- PROBLEM-SOLVING INSTITUTION -- PROBLEM-SOLVING CIVILIZATION." (S005)
- On the corporate speech he keeps mocking: the JAZA call to arms (workforce skills for the AI economy) is real to him, but the reaction matters more: "OF COURSE HE'D SAY THAT... We have all sat through this speech before. THE QUESTION IS: What were Philippine business leaders really worried about that night?" He respects the substance and despises the ritual. (S005)
- On collaboration rhetoric: "NOT 'TOGETHER', THE FEEL-GOOD BLAH BLAH -- BUT THE KIND OF TOGETHER THAT RADICALLY CHANGES THE MATH FOR EACH OF US." (S005)

## On scale and ambition

- His expeditions scale deliberately: "EXPEDITION 2025: 40 VCs & FOUNDERS. EXPEDITION 2026: 300 TALENTS. EXPEDITION 2027: 25,000 PROBLEM SOLVERS. EXPEDITION 2028: GOLD STANDARD FOR TALENT. EXPEDITION 2029: FILIPINOS AT GLOBAL FOREFRONT." (S005)
- The test of the whole model: "can 25,000 radically different people become more capable individually because they are solving consequential problems collectively -- and can society become better at attacking its hardest problems because those people exist?" (S005)
- "Every Expedition should make the next one stronger." (S005)

## On what he funds and builds

- Sector spread: "venture capital, technology, media, education, healthcare, energy, hospitality, food, agri, HR and enterprise solutions." (S003)
- The 100X challenges he curates are economic-first: AI x human value, the first big economic leap of rising households, AI x small business, food and agri, the geography of opportunity, health before sickness, gig work without the old safety net, the channel rewrite. (S005)
- On underpriced terrain: "Choose the terrain that is moving fastest around you" -- AI x work, small business, rising middle class, urban concentration, digital-native income. (S005)
- On installed advantages: "What do you already have that could change the game? Customers, distribution, data, employees, physical footprint, merchants/agents, logistics, procurement ecosystems." (S005)

## On spirituality and mission (do not skip this)

- The Manifesto was "a prayer, handed down, gifted, entrusted." (S002)
- "I see innovation as my humbling participation in God's ongoing work of creation." (S003)
- GPS is for him inseparable from service: "what I have been given, I must give and live for others." (S002)
