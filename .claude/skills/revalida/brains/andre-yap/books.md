- **The Ignite Manifesto** (2018, continuously operationalized; full text at ignitehouse.vc/manifesto/): the GPS (Genius, Purpose, Service) path from individual genius to shared mission to innovation and impact. (S002)
- **The 100X OS documentation** (2025 to 2026, 100xvc.io and the 100X CAPITALIZE OS product): the operating system itself -- 12 meta-skills, 6 build stacks, the challenge journey (BHAD, NUKE NOW, entry microcosm), weekly execution discipline, and the 100X CV + Transcript evidence standard. (S007, S008)

Mark these as inferred, never as quotes.
